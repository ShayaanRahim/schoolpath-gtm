# SA School Analytics sample — what it tells us (and what it doesn't)

Source: the three CSV exports (3,282 posts, 9,924 comments, 12,429 members) from 9 San Antonio Facebook groups, May 2025 – Aug 2026.

## Headline numbers

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Posts about ages 0–5 (daycare, pre-K, preschool, infant, toddler, kinder, Montessori) | 305 of 3,282 (9.3%) |

## Where the data falls short

1. **Wrong geography mix.** The groups skew west side: Helotes and Alamo Ranch dominate. Only one group (`14695971124`) is Stone Oak-centric, and **no group in the sample covers Schertz/Cibolo/Garden Ridge**. The Northeast corridor is invisible here.
2. **Neighborhood groups are low-density.** The Stone Oak group has 566 posts but only ~1% are about early schooling; much of that is provider ads (home daycares, even a floor cleaner). General "living in X" groups are mostly noise.
3. **The one high-density group is citywide and charter-focused.** SA Charter Moms Discussion (11,000+ members per Community Impact) has 20% early-ed content, but it's about charter K, not daycare.
4. **Daycare/infant is underrepresented** (33 posts). Those parents likely ask somewhere else: subdivision groups, Nextdoor, military spouse groups, Peanut/The Bump.

## What parents ask and how they say it

- Vocabulary: "kinder", "prek"/"pre-k", "recommendations", "experience with", "thoughts on", "half day", "Montessori", "dual language/Spanish", "outdoor time", "small classes", "waitlist", "tour".
- Recurring needs: half-day vs full-day, Montessori/play-based, Spanish immersion, special needs (autism, ADHD, IEP), structure/rigor, uniforms and homework at a specific charter.
- Schools most named in replies to early-ed asks: Montessori programs (113), Great Hearts (55), BASIS (53), SST (47), NEISD (43), Pre-K 4 SA (32), Founders Classical Schertz, Bezos Academy (Universal City), Keystone.
- Decision is often framed as a **comparison** ("Founders Schertz vs Great Hearts Live Oak", "GHNO vs staying at Encino Park"). Best reply = side-by-side, links to both profiles.

## Evidence for the military lane

- "Private preschool for an autism level 2 / non-speaking 4 year old. We will be near Randolph." (June 2025, 35 comments)

- 49 posts mention Randolph/JBSA/military/PCS.

## Evidence that disclosure works here

Disclosed institutional voices are normal and accepted: "I work for CAST Schools…", "I work for the enrollment office, feel free to contact me", "I work at GH". Parents also openly vouch "real human being (not a bot)", so bot suspicion exists, but disclosed humans are welcome.

## Seasonality — the biggest risk to the 4-week target

Early-ed asks by month (citywide, this sample): Feb 18 · Mar 13 · Apr 19 · May 28 · Jun 31 · Jul 31 · Aug 31 · Sep 17 · **Oct 10 · Nov 10** · Jan 2.

A November run lands in the trough for "recommend a daycare" posts. But November–January is **application season** (SAISD's window opens Nov 3 per a comment in the data; charter lotteries, Pre-K 4 SA, Bezos Academy, private-school open houses). So the November hook is "applications/open houses are opening", which maps to SchoolPath's **Events** calendar and school profiles, not just the match quiz.

## Implications for the system

- Don't rely on general neighborhood groups; prioritize parent/mom groups, military spouse/PCS groups, SA Charter Moms, Reddit, Nextdoor.
- Add a **seed-post / proactive** play for the trough: disclosed "open house & application deadline roundup for North SA" posts.
- Filter provider ads aggressively (≈6–18% promo content per group).
- Replies compete with ~7 other comments: they must be specific (named schools, half-day/Montessori/Spanish filters) to stand out.
